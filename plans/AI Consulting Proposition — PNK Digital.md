# AI Consulting Proposition — PNK Digital

Oct 20, 2026 · @Mike

## Summary

Sell a productised, fixed-price path from "we don't know where to start" to "one AI workflow live and measured", for UK firms of 10–100 staff, led by a developer who builds rather than a strategist who leaves slides behind. The ladder is: free 30-minute call → AI Opportunity Audit (£1,950) → Pilot Build (from £6,500) → Fractional AI Lead retainer (£2,750–£4,500/month). The wedge against the hundreds of new "AI consultants" is 27 years shipping software in financial services plus daily hands-on use of agentic tooling: you can scope it, build it, and leave the client owning it.

## What the market is asking for in late 2026

The demand is for someone to turn scattered ChatGPT use into a working process; the blocker is skills, not budget. Adoption is rising fast but stays shallow: the official ONS rate went from 10% (late 2023) to 25% (December 2025), and 54% of Chamber-member SMEs say they use AI (March 2026), yet 75% of adopters report productivity gains while only 12% report higher revenue, and agentic AI sits at just 7% of adopters ([Whito compilation of ONS/DSIT/BCC data](https://whito.co.uk/research/uk-ai-adoption-statistics/), updated 12 Oct 2026).

| Signal | Figure | What it means for the offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skills gap is the top barrier | 46% of small firms say they lack the knowledge to use AI (FSB); \~60% of businesses cite limited AI skills (DSIT) | Sell capability transfer and a named first workflow, not "strategy" |
| Nobody has identified a use case | 71% hadn't found a clear use for AI (DSIT); 30% of non-adopters see no value (YouGov) | The entry product must be a diagnostic that ends with a ranked list, priced to be a no-brainer |
| Trust and data concerns | 49% of non-adopters cite privacy/security; 73% of small firms have concerns (FSB) | Lead with UK GDPR, data boundaries and "human in the loop" on the page |
| Usage is shallow | 85% of adopters use text generation; 7% use agents; \~11% of SMEs automate operations to a great extent | The gap between chat and workflow automation is the product |
| Productivity without revenue | 75% see productivity gains, 12% see revenue gains | Measure hours saved and what they were reinvested in; it's the proof the page needs |
| Sector skew | Info/comms 43% adoption; professional services next; construction, hospitality, retail \~10% | Target professional and financial services first; they adopt and can pay |

Three shifts since 2025 matter for positioning. Buyers now ask for fixed prices and a free first step: eight of ten UK SME-focused AI consultancies publish a free audit or scoping call ([Clever Merchants survey, Sept 2026](https://clevermerchants.com/blog/best-ai-consultants-uk/)). "Fractional AI officer" has become a recognised retainer category, with title inflation already drawing criticism ([Lilach Bullock, Oct 2026](https://www.lilachbullock.com/fractional-ai-officer-vs-ai-consultant-difference/)). And agent builds are being priced from £750 to £15,000 at small agencies, so an advisor who can also build sits in the gap between "consultant who leaves a plan" and "agency that needs a £20k minimum" ([FactoryJet cost guide, 9 Oct 2026](https://factoryjet.com/blog/ai-cost-uk-2026)).

## Positioning: why PNK Digital

The angle is "the AI consultant who ships": a senior engineer who has used AI to deliver real software for regulated clients, not a marketer who learned prompting last year. Most competitors fall into two camps, and the page should say so plainly. Strategy consultants produce a roadmap and leave, so nothing gets built. Automation agencies build, but need a £8k–£25k minimum and don't understand the client's domain or regulatory exposure.

Four proof points to put on the page:

1. **27 years of software delivery, mostly in financial services.** Claims, life insurance and pensions are high-compliance, data-sensitive domains; if AI can be deployed safely there, it can be deployed in a solicitor's, an accountancy or a broker.
2. **Builds with AI every day.** Agentic coding workflows, multi-agent setups, self-hosted models and RAG stacks are part of normal delivery, not a demo. This answers the "do you actually know how to do it?" doubt.
3. **Vendor-neutral and build-or-buy honest.** No reseller margin on any tool; the audit will say when an off-the-shelf £30/month tool beats a build.
4. **Fixed prices, you own everything.** Code, prompts, accounts and documentation stay with the client; no lock-in retainer required to keep what was built.

One line to anchor the page: *"I find the three processes AI can take off your team, build the first one, and leave you able to run it."*

This sits alongside the existing [tech-support plans](https://pnk-digital.com/tech-support): same brand promise (a senior person you can reach, fixed monthly cost), a different buyer (owner/MD rather than office manager). Cross-sell runs both ways: support clients are warm leads for an audit, and audit clients who need ongoing hands become retainer or support clients.

## Who it's for

Target owner-led firms of 10–100 staff in professional and financial services, where there is repetitive document and inbox work, a compliance reason to be careful, and a budget owner who can sign off £2k–£10k without a committee. Trades, hospitality and retail are left for the support plans; adoption there is around 10% and the audit is a harder sell.

## Service tiers

Four steps, each with a fixed price, a named output and a decision point; nobody is asked to commit to the next step until the current one has paid. The free call filters, the audit converts, the pilot proves, the retainer compounds.

| 3. Pilot Build | One workflow built on the client's existing tools, human-approval step included, 2 training sessions, written runbook, 30-day measurement of hours saved; client owns code, prompts and accounts | 3–5 weeks | 8–12 days | From £6,500; typical £6,500–£12,000 |
| 4. Fractional AI Lead | 2–4 days a month: run the roadmap, next workflows, vendor filter, staff drop-in clinic, monthly adoption report to the owner; 3-month minimum, then rolling monthly | Ongoing | 2–4 days/mo | £2,750/mo (2 days) or £4,500/mo (4 days) |

Two add-ons for the page, priced separately so they don't dilute the core ladder:

- **Team AI Workshop** — half day, hands-on with the client's own documents and tools, plus a one-page AI use policy they can adopt. £1,250. Works as a standalone lead product for firms not ready for an audit, and as a follow-on after a pilot.
- **AI Delivery Setup for dev teams** — install an agentic coding workflow (Claude Code, subagents, conventions, review gates) in an engineering team and pair with them for a sprint. From £7,500. Markets to the "SaaS and dev-led" segment only.

What is deliberately not offered: open-ended day-rate advisory (it invites comparison with the £564 contract median), enterprise-scale programmes, and reselling any vendor's tool.

Price the audit under £2,000, the pilot in the £6k–£12k band and the retainer at an effective £1,125–£1,375 a day. **Why these exact numbers**

- £1,950 audit: sits just above the "free-to-£2,000" small-agency band so it reads as serious, under the £3,000 boutique cluster so an owner can sign it alone, and the credit-against-pilot removes the risk of paying twice. At 3 days' work it's £650/day, acceptable for a lead product.
- £6,500 pilot floor: matches where small agencies price a one-system agent, undercuts the £8k software-firm floor, and at 8–12 days yields £540–£810/day plus the audit credit. Price the quote per pilot from the audit's scope; publish "from £6,500, typical £6,500–£12,000".
- £2,750 / £4,500 retainer: bottom-to-middle of the £2,500–£8,000 small-business fractional band, which is where a 10–100 person firm actually buys; it's £1,125–£1,375 a day, the best yield in the ladder and the thing to steer towards.
- £1,250 workshop: UK training workshops publish at £750–£1,500 for a half or full day; the client's-own-documents format justifies the top of that.

**Pricing rules for the page**

- Publish the numbers. Most UK competitors don't, and buyers in this segment compare on price certainty, not prestige.
- Quote ex VAT with "+VAT" shown; most target clients are VAT-registered and reclaim it.
- Keep the audit credit at 30 days to create a deadline without discounting.
- Review quarterly against ITJobsWatch; rates have drifted down 2–6% year on year, so don't anchor to 2025 guides.

Assumption: all pricing is in GBP ex VAT and sized for 10–100 staff firms; multi-site or regulated-data audits should be quoted individually above £3,500.

## Landing page blueprint

One page at pnk-digital.com/ai-consulting, built in the Astro site alongside /tech-support, one primary CTA (book the free AI Fit Call) repeated four times, and the three prices visible without scrolling past the fold on mobile. Sections in order:

1. **Hero.** Headline: *"AI that actually does the work. Built into your business by someone who's shipped software for 27 years."* Sub: *"I find the three processes AI can take off your team, build the first one, and leave you able to run it. Fixed prices. You own everything."* CTA: Book a free 30-minute AI Fit Call. Secondary link: See prices.
2. **The problem, in their words.** Three short cards: "Everyone's using ChatGPT, nothing has changed"; "We don't know which process to start with"; "We can't risk client data in a tool we don't understand". One stat under each (71% haven't found a clear use; 7% use agents; 49% cite data privacy), each linked to its source.
3. **How it works.** The four-step ladder as a horizontal strip: Fit Call → Audit → Pilot → Fractional Lead, with time, output and price on each step. Note the audit credit.
4. **What a pilot looks like.** One worked example from the target segments (e.g. a broker's client-review prep: before 90 minutes per client, after 15 with a human check), with hours saved per month and the implied payback. Replace with a real case after the first pilot.
5. **Why me, not an agency or a strategist.** The four proof points from Positioning, plus a comparison strip: Strategy consultant (plan, no build) / Automation agency (build, £20k+ minimum, no domain) / PNK Digital (scope, build, hand over, from £1,950).
6. **Safe by design.** UK GDPR, data stays in the client's accounts, human-approval step on every workflow that touches a customer, written AI use policy included in every audit. Short; this is the trust block for regulated buyers.
7. **Pricing.** Three cards: Audit £1,950 / Pilot from £6,500 / Fractional Lead from £2,750 per month; workshop and dev-team setup as a line under them. All +VAT.
8. **Proof.** Testimonials: ask Top Gardeners and Baik Consulting for a line each on reliability and plain English (they speak to the person, not the AI service), and secure one AI-specific quote from the first audit client. Logos of the sectors served rather than named insurers under NDA.
9. **FAQ.** Six questions: "Do we need to buy new software?"; "What if AI isn't right for us?" (you'll say so in the audit); "Who owns what we build?"; "Is our data used to train anything?"; "How is this different from our IT support?"; "Can we stop the retainer?".
10. **Final CTA + calendar embed.**

Supporting assets to build at the same time: a Calendly-style booking page for the Fit Call, a one-page audit sample (anonymised) as a PDF download in exchange for an email, and a three-email follow-up for people who book but don't convert. Track one metric: Fit Calls booked per week.

A/B test, as with the support page: version A is "Mike, senior engineer who builds with AI" (first person, photo); version B is "PNK Digital, AI consultancy". Expect A to win in this segment; buyers at 10–100 staff are buying a person.

## Go-to-market: first 90 days

Sell two audits before polishing anything; the first paid audit produces the case study, the testimonial and the pilot that make the page convert. Momentum is the known risk, so each phase has one measurable gate.

**Weeks 1–3: ship the offer**

- [ ] Write the audit template (interview script, process map, use-case register with scoring, report skeleton) so delivery is repeatable from day one
- [ ] Build /ai-consulting in Astro from the blueprint; publish prices; booking link live
- [ ] Draft the AI use policy template that ships with every audit
- [ ] Gate: page live and one anonymised sample report ready

**Weeks 3–8: first two clients**

- [ ] Offer the audit at £1,950 to three warm contacts (support-plan clients, Guardian network, the broker client) with the pilot credit
- [ ] 20 personalised LinkedIn messages a week to owners/MDs of brokers, accountancies and solicitors within an hour of home; lead with one specific process, not "AI"
- [ ] Run one free 45-minute "What AI can take off your desk" session for a local business network or Chamber; the workshop format doubles as the sales pitch
- [ ] Gate: two paid audits delivered, one pilot quoted

**Weeks 8–13: proof and repeat**

- [ ] Deliver the first pilot; measure hours saved for 30 days; write it up as the page's worked example
- [ ] Convert one pilot client to the Fractional AI Lead retainer
- [ ] Publish two short posts a month on LinkedIn from real audit findings (anonymised)
- [ ] Gate: £10k invoiced from AI consulting and one retainer signed

Capacity check: at full load the ladder is roughly 2 audits (6 days) + 1 pilot (10 days) + 1 retainer (3 days) a month, which is 19 days and only works if contracting is reduced to 2–3 days a week. Decide at the week-13 gate whether the AI work has earned that shift.
