## What this list tells us

1. **The 30-150 band is genuinely thin in Surrey/Hampshire.** The lower bound rules out most "interesting" recent startups (typical SEGF cohort) and the upper bound rules out the established firms that show up in news. Real fits are quiet B2B SMEs not making press.
2. **The right next-tool isn't web search — it's Companies House SIC-code + region search.** Filter to specific SIC codes (62.02 IT consultancy, 70.22 management consultancy, 69.20 accountancy, 66.22 insurance brokering, 49.41 freight, 86.21 medical practice) in Surrey/Hampshire postcodes with 30-150 employees on most recent accounts. This is mechanical and would surface 100+ candidates quickly.
3. **Surrey Research Park (140-200 tenants) hasn't been deeply explored.** Confirmed Life Sciences tenants worth investigating: Datar Cancer Genetics, Conidia Bioscience, MedPharm, Occupational Health Assessment Ltd.
4. **4 confirmed leads is enough to test the outreach.** Don't keep researching — try emailing these 4 first. If 0/4 reply after a follow-up, the pitch is the problem and more prospects won't help. If 1+/4 reply, that proves the channel and *then* invest in more research.
